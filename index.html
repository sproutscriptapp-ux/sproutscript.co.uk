Our story

Built at our kitchen table, not in a boardroom.

SproutScript wasn't designed by a tech company guessing what childminders need. It was designed by a registered childminder who spent years losing every evening to daily diaries and EYFS write-ups — and finally made her husband build the app she actually wanted.

Every feature exists because she needed it in her own setting. And when something annoys you, it probably annoys her too — so it gets fixed.

They have ticket queues. We have a childminder at the dinner table.

Your whole setting, in one place

Built around what a real childminding day actually involves.

🌱

EYFS progress, tracked together

A full EYFS tick list for every child, with notes that both you and parents can add — so development tracking is a shared conversation, not a solo write-up.

📖

Daily records parents can see

Attendance, activities, meals and photos, logged as you go and shared with parents — everything in one place when the inspection call comes.

💷

Your money, sorted

A built-in financial module with receipt logging and income & expenditure tracking — so tax-return season stops being a shoebox of paper.

How it works

Log as you go

Attendance, meals, activities, photos and EYFS ticks — recorded in moments during the day, not reconstructed from memory at night.

Parents join in

Parents see their child's records and can add their own notes to the EYFS tracking — development becomes a two-way conversation.

Evenings are yours again

Records filed, receipts logged, income and expenditure up to date. Close the app and put the kettle on — the 7pm pile is gone.
